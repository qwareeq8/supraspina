Latin Modern Roman source manifest for Supraspina
===============================================

Supraspina bundles four complete, byte-identical Latin Modern Roman 10 version
2.004 OpenType/CFF faces from the official CTAN distribution. Their embedded
family is `LM Roman 10`. The embedded copyright is "Copyright 2003, 2009 B.
Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX users groups)." The GUST Font
License 1.0 or later applies; complete terms are in
`licenses/GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt`.

Each source URL uses CTAN's canonical mirror redirect. Verify the downloaded
bytes against the immutable size and SHA-256 before replacing a bundled face.
